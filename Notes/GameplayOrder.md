On start, player can access Emberglen, Glasswater, Cinderrail, and Gloam Quarry. Dragon Keep is closed.

Gloam Quarry is only accessed via Cinderrail, and a direct shortcut from Cinderrail to Emberglen is unlocked only after reaching the quarry.

After the player collects 3 shards, and gives them to Mercer, the Veiled One appears. After the Veiled One battle, the player can challenge Aurelia for the first crest.

Beating Aurelia opens the player up to Act II.

In Act II, the player is given a quest to access the darkness seal in gloam quarry. Over there, Sister Vey teaches the player to enter the darkness dungeon. Upon beating the boss there, the player unlocks the nightvision ability, and can see in dark caves.

Darkness is the first seal that the player can access. After completing the darkness seal, the player can then access the Cinderrail, Rootmaze, Glasswater crest battles, and also the Fire and Nature seals.

Each seal opens up a dungeon area. Beating the boss of the dungeon gives the player a card reward which he can use for map traversal to previously locked areas.

Darkness -> <some darkness card> -> Nightvision -> Can see inside dark caves
Nature -> Xeno Mantis -> Cut -> Player can cut down bushes
Fire -> Smashwarrior Stragandu -> Rock Smash -> Player can break rocks
Water -> Aqua Surfer -> Surf -> Player can traverse water bodies
Light -> ??? -> ???

After completing the 3 crest battles and the fire/nature seals, Dragon Keep opens up. Here the player can beat Kestrel and continue the Hollow storyline.

After this, the entire map is open. The player has to beat the remaining 3 crest battles and complete the light and water seals. This leads to hollow storyline finale, then the final crest battle vs Caelum Rhos, followed by the Championship.
