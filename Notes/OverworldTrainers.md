# Overworld Trainer Mini-Profiles

## Purpose and reward loop

These are optional, low-story NPCs who populate roads, arenas, shops, and side paths. They provide frequent duels, collection growth, and gold between Crest Holder encounters. They may comment on local events, but they do not carry clues or make decisions that affect the main plot.

When the player enters a trainer's short line of sight, the trainer displays an exclamation mark and walks over to issue a challenge. Losing does not block the route. Afterward, the player may initiate rematches by speaking to the trainer.

Each trainer follows the existing four-win structure:

- Each of the first four victories awards the listed gold and one copy of the featured card.
- The first rematch introduces an upgraded deck or secondary civilization.
- After four victories, the player owns a full playset and the trainer switches to friendly post-battle dialogue.
- Gold values below are initial balancing targets and can be adjusted after playtesting.

Sprite designs should reuse common trainer bases while changing hair, palette, hats, tools, and posture. This keeps production manageable without returning to identical-looking NPCs.

## Emberglen Roads

These trainers introduce the collection loop before the player leaves Act I. Their decks are straightforward and their reward cards help construct basic decks.

### Pip — Festival Page

- **Visual:** Short cream festival coat, oversized red sash, and a stack of entry forms.
- **Deck:** Light/Fire low-cost attackers supported by tapping effects.
- **Challenge:** “Official festival business! Which currently means dueling everyone I can find.”
- **Reward:** 40 gold and **Miele, Vizier of Lightning** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds several Fire speed attackers and becomes more willing to attack shields early.

### Noma — Canal Runner

- **Visual:** Blue cap, rolled trousers, messenger pouch, and wet boots.
- **Deck:** Water creatures, card draw, and simple bounce effects.
- **Challenge:** “I can deliver this parcel late, or I can duel you now. Easy decision.”
- **Reward:** 45 gold and **Aqua Hulcus** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Light blockers so the deck can protect its growing hand.

### Bram — Orchard Hand

- **Visual:** Straw hat, green work shirt, pruning hook, and muddy knees.
- **Deck:** Nature mana acceleration into medium Beast Folk.
- **Challenge:** “The trees need time to grow. My mana zone usually needs less.”
- **Reward:** 45 gold and **Bronze-Arm Tribe** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds evolution creatures and stops overcharging useful cards into mana.

## Sunspire Cloister

Sunspire trainers emphasize defense and deliberate combat. They are encountered during the Light Resonance Seal investigation even though the area has no Circuit Crest Holder.

### Elia — Novice Warden

- **Visual:** White novice robe, square gold shoulder pads, and a shield-shaped satchel.
- **Deck:** Mono-Light blockers with a small tapping package.
- **Challenge:** “The first duty of a warden is to stand firm. The second is to know when to move.”
- **Reward:** 60 gold and **La Ura Giga, Sky Guardian** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds larger Guardians and saves blockers that would make poor attacks.

### Tomas — Bellkeeper

- **Visual:** Pale blue cloak, heavy gloves, and two brass handbells at his belt.
- **Deck:** Light/Water control using blockers, tapping, and card draw.
- **Challenge:** “Hear that echo? The bell says you have time for one duel.”
- **Reward:** 65 gold and **Mist Rias, Sonic Guardian** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Water bounce effects and better protects Mist Rias after summoning it.

### Senn — Road Pilgrim

- **Visual:** Weathered tan cloak over white clothes, walking staff, and many small travel charms.
- **Deck:** Light/Nature creature swarm that turns small bodies into attackers.
- **Challenge:** “Every road teaches something. This one appears to teach us who wins.”
- **Reward:** 65 gold and **Diamond Cutter** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Nature acceleration and waits for a wider board before using Diamond Cutter.

## Cinderrail Foundry

Cinderrail trainers favor fast turns and aggressive evolution lines. Their work tools double as recognizable sprite accessories.

### Kipp — Riveter

- **Visual:** Orange goggles, sleeveless red work coat, and a compact riveting hammer.
- **Deck:** Fire Humans leading into Armored Blaster evolutions.
- **Challenge:** “One clean hit joins steel. Let's see what one clean attack does to your shields.”
- **Reward:** 75 gold and **Engineer Kipo** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Light blockers to preserve evolution material for later turns.

### Ansa — Rail Courier

- **Visual:** Long red scarf, green courier jacket, and a timetable clipped to one wrist.
- **Deck:** Fire/Nature Dragon acceleration using mana growth and cost support.
- **Challenge:** “The express does not wait, and neither do my dragons.”
- **Reward:** 80 gold and **Cocco Lupia** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds larger Dragons and becomes more selective about which cards enter mana.

### Holt — Furnace Guard

- **Visual:** Dark protective coat, pale metal helmet, and a square furnace shield.
- **Deck:** Fire/Light control with blockers, mass removal, and a few heavy attackers.
- **Challenge:** “Heat is easy. Containment is the skill. Show me yours.”
- **Reward:** 80 gold and **Burst Shot** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds anti-blocker creatures and avoids using Burst Shot when it would lose the better board.

## Glasswater Port

Glasswater trainers manipulate cards in hand and on the field. Several patrol docks or narrow canal bridges, making them natural line-of-sight encounters.

### Pell — Dockhand

- **Visual:** Teal knit cap, rope over one shoulder, and striped waterproof trousers.
- **Deck:** Water blockers, hand filtering, and shield-trigger creatures.
- **Challenge:** “Cargo shifts. Tides shift. A good deck shifts before either one.”
- **Reward:** 85 gold and **Emeral** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Darkness removal and uses Emeral to prepare stronger shield triggers.

### Iri — Ledger Broker

- **Visual:** Violet waistcoat, blue spectacles, and a waterproof account book.
- **Deck:** Water/Darkness discard and bounce focused on reducing the opponent's options.
- **Challenge:** “Cards in hand are assets. I intend to audit yours.”
- **Reward:** 90 gold and **Corile** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds hand-size payoffs and uses discard before returning threats to the deck.

### Sol — Chartmaker

- **Visual:** Wide blue rain hat, rolled maps, and a green compass case.
- **Deck:** Water/Nature search, mana development, and adaptable single-card answers.
- **Challenge:** “I have mapped every route through the port except the one your deck will take.”
- **Reward:** 90 gold and **Crystal Memory** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Light shield triggers and searches for answers based on the current board.

## Rootmaze Commons

Rootmaze trainers use creature families and evolution chains. Their sprites should incorporate leaves, field equipment, or small creature-care tools without making them look like major wardens.

### Fern — Trail Forager

- **Visual:** Green hood, berry baskets, and a belt crowded with labeled pouches.
- **Deck:** Nature Beast Folk swarm and evolution support.
- **Challenge:** “I was looking for moonberries, but a good duel is nearly as rare.”
- **Reward:** 95 gold and **Barkwhip, the Smasher** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Water draw so the swarm can recover after removal.

### Toma — Beetle Keeper

- **Visual:** Brown apron, oversized leaf gloves, and a tiny wooden insect carrier.
- **Deck:** Nature/Darkness creatures that benefit from destruction and graveyard setup.
- **Challenge:** “Don't worry. The beetles only become agitated when they sense hesitation.”
- **Reward:** 95 gold and **Essence Elf** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Worms and deliberate sacrifice effects instead of attacking every creature immediately.

### Moss — Path Warden

- **Visual:** Green-and-blue poncho, wooden direction signs strapped to a pack, and one red boot.
- **Deck:** Nature/Water toolbox using search, mana acceleration, and bounce.
- **Challenge:** “Wrong turn. The way back is free; the way forward costs one duel.”
- **Reward:** 100 gold and **Dimension Gate** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds evolution finishers and searches for creature types needed by the current hand.

## Gloam Quarry

Gloam trainers introduce more demanding sacrifice and graveyard strategies. Their dialogue can be solemn or dryly funny without displacing Sister Vey's Ashen Crest trial or Hollow-story conflict.

### Rell — Lamplighter

- **Visual:** Tall black cap, pale coat, and a purple glass lantern on a hooked pole.
- **Deck:** Darkness/Light blockers and sacrifice creatures.
- **Challenge:** “The lamps keep the path visible. Your graveyard will keep the duel interesting.”
- **Reward:** 105 gold and **Stinger Worm** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds stronger destruction payoffs and always completes mandatory sacrifice choices.

### Dema — Quarry Blaster

- **Visual:** Red ear protectors, charcoal work vest, and a bundle of harmless blasting flags.
- **Deck:** Darkness/Fire graveyard recursion with fast attackers.
- **Challenge:** “Stand behind the marker. Unless your deck is afraid of a little noise.”
- **Reward:** 110 gold and **Jack Viper, Shadow of Doom** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds more Chimera creatures and preserves them when Jack Viper can create a stronger recursion turn.

### Ivo — Epitaph Keeper

- **Visual:** Neat gray coat, violet scarf, chalk-covered gloves, and a tablet of names.
- **Deck:** Darkness/Water control using removal, draw, and graveyard recovery.
- **Challenge:** “I record memorable defeats. Would you care to contribute mine or yours?”
- **Reward:** 110 gold and **Terror Pit** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds hand disruption and saves removal for creatures that materially threaten the board.

## Confluence Roads

Confluence trainers use three civilizations and more coherent deck archetypes. They prepare the player for Kestrel's mixed deck rather than simply combining unrelated strong cards.

### Qia — Route Tinker

- **Visual:** Blue mechanic coat, green tool belt, and a red pinwheel used to test wind direction.
- **Deck:** Nature/Water mana toolbox using creature swapping and bounce.
- **Challenge:** “Every route works after enough adjustments. Decks too.”
- **Reward:** 115 gold and **Soulswap** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Darkness recursion and evaluates the value of both creatures before swapping them.

### Jori — Ferry Captain

- **Visual:** Navy captain's cap, orange life vest, and a polished brass whistle.
- **Deck:** Water/Fire/Light shield-trigger control with sudden counterattacks.
- **Challenge:** “Crossing is free today. The captain's duel is not optional—well, mostly not.”
- **Reward:** 120 gold and **Aqua Surfer** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds shield setup and holds fast attackers until bounce effects create an opening.

### Fen — Caravan Cook

- **Visual:** Red bandanna, green apron, blue cooking pot, and a wooden spoon held like a baton.
- **Deck:** Fire/Nature/Water Dragon ramp with draw support.
- **Challenge:** “A pinch of Fire, a scoop of Nature, Water as needed. Same recipe for stew and decks.”
- **Reward:** 120 gold and **Magmadragon Melgars** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds larger Dragons and refuses to charge its only source of a required civilization.

## Mirror Arena District

Mirror District trainers are competitive Circuit regulars rather than local workers. Their decks test adaptation but remain weaker and more predictable than Sable Meridian's variants.

### Lume — Stage Duelist

- **Visual:** Split red-and-white jacket, theatrical half-mask, and dramatic swept hair.
- **Deck:** Fire/Light Humans, tapping, and evolution attacks.
- **Challenge:** “Every duel needs an entrance. Mine starts when you lose the first shield.”
- **Reward:** 130 gold and **Craze Valkyrie, the Drastic** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Water draw and delays evolution until it can exploit a tapped board.

### Tess — Match Recorder

- **Visual:** Blue hood, violet notebook, and a compact crystal recording lens.
- **Deck:** Water/Darkness evasive attackers backed by discard.
- **Challenge:** “Your previous matches suggest three likely openings. Please choose the interesting one.”
- **Reward:** 135 gold and **Melnia, the Aqua Shadow** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds Light blockers and adapts its attack targets to the player's available blockers.

### Varo — Arena Skeptic

- **Visual:** Gray-green coat, crossed-arm stance, and a badge reading `PROVE IT`.
- **Deck:** Water/Light/Darkness control that wins through accumulated card advantage.
- **Challenge:** “Everyone arriving here thinks their deck is special. Demonstrations are accepted.”
- **Reward:** 135 gold and **Marinomancer** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Adds civilization-sensitive effects and improves its mana-color planning.

## Crown Gate Approach

These are late-game trainers who already hold several Crests. They have strong legal decks and higher gold rewards, but no role in deciding the championship or resolving the Hollow crisis.

### Fara — Circuit Scout

- **Visual:** White travel coat, green shoulder scarf, five small Crest replicas, and binoculars.
- **Deck:** Light/Nature creature-family support and resilient board development.
- **Challenge:** “Seven Crests between us. Only one way to decide whose route was better.”
- **Reward:** 150 gold and **Petrova, Channeler of Suns** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Changes its supported creature family and adds targeted removal for opposing blockers.

### Odo — Arena Veteran

- **Visual:** Faded blue dueling coat, silver beard, and an old championship ticket tucked into his hat.
- **Deck:** Light/Water/Darkness control with spell recovery and efficient shield triggers.
- **Challenge:** “I have been one match from the Crown Gate six times. That makes me experienced, not bitter.”
- **Reward:** 155 gold and **Phal Eega, Dawn Guardian** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Recovers high-value spells more carefully and stops attacking into obviously unfavorable trades.

### Rhun — Gate Challenger

- **Visual:** Black sleeveless coat over blue-and-green clothes, wrist wraps, and an overfilled deck case.
- **Deck:** Darkness/Water/Nature evolution toolbox with graveyard recovery.
- **Challenge:** “Caelum says my deck has too many plans. I say it has exactly enough to beat yours.”
- **Reward:** 160 gold and **Tanzanyte, the Awakener** per victory.
- **Rematch:** Removes its weakest package, improves civilization consistency, and becomes a preview of championship-level AI.

## Implementation notes

- Minor trainers should not appear in the story objective tracker.
- Their dialogue needs only challenge, victory, defeat, rematch, and completed states.
- Trainers can share movement behavior and sprite bodies with palette and accessory variants.
- Their AI may reuse HeuristicBot with a small archetype profile rather than requiring unique behavior classes.
- At least one trainer per region should stand off the critical path so exploration produces tangible collection rewards.
- Route trainers should never inspect hidden opposing cards, even when their characterization emphasizes prediction or analysis.
