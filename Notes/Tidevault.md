# Tidevault

## Role and identity

Tidevault is a required late-game water town and the sanctuary of the Water
Resonance Seal. It opens with the outer road network after the Confluence relay
is restored. Glasswater Port keeps the Tidal Crest, arena, and stolen-prediction
investigation; Tidevault holds the actual calibration standard that those
predictions have corrupted.

The town occupies terraces around a deep blue headwater basin. Rain from the
Stormbreak highlands collects here, passes through ancient pressure galleries,
and eventually feeds the underground river beneath Glasswater. Tidevault should
feel quieter and older than the port: slate roofs, white spillways, blue lamps,
stepped pools, and massive gates whose motion is visible throughout town.

**Planned region ID:** `tidevault`

**Common decks:** Water control, Liquid People evolution, Cyber Lord card
selection, Leviathan finishers, and mixed Water/Light or Water/Nature decks
built around protecting a plan until the correct moment.

## Background

Tidevault was founded around a naturally stable point in the Water current.
The first keepers discovered that reflections in the basin preserved not a
single future, but the range of routes still available after each choice. They
built the Water Resonance Seal to record that capacity for informed change.

Glasswater grew much later, downstream. Its engineers connected tide gauges and
canal controls to a public relay of Tidevault's readings. Over generations,
people shortened “Water Seal relay” to “the Seal,” leading many outsiders—and
some modern Glasswater officials—to assume the sanctuary sat beneath the port.
The actual Seal has always remained at Tidevault.

Tidevault's keepers deliberately limited the relay. It could compare public
flows and voluntarily submitted decisions, but it could not inspect private
memories. Glasswater's prediction project broke that rule, then sent its stolen
data upstream as if it were valid calibration evidence. The Curator amplified
the false input until the Water Seal began treating coerced certainty as healthy
adaptation.

## Layout

```text
                   Rainstep Gate to Stormbreak
                              |
                      Upper Spillway Ward
                              |
        Keeper Steps ---- Basin Forum ---- Current Market
              |               |                  |
        Seal Archive ---- Bluewell Lift ---- Pilgrim Quay
              |               |                  |
         Reflection Walk ---- Lower Locks ---- Tideglass Gate
                                                 |
                                  Tideglass Aqueduct to Glasswater
```

The town has two reliable north-south routes: the public terraces around Basin
Forum and the service path along the spillways. Bridges across the basin create
short loops and viewpoints, while lifts provide optional shortcuts only. No
required path should depend on waiting for a water level or moving platform.

### Tideglass Gate

The southern entrance receives the covered aqueduct and maintenance road from
Glasswater. Before the Confluence relay opens, a physical pressure checkpoint
blocks passage beyond the Glasswater side. Afterward it remains permanently
open and bidirectional.

Glasswater's recovered consent ledger is checked here on the first story visit.
It proves that the corrupted readings came from unauthorized match memories,
but does not solve what Tidevault should do with data already absorbed by the
Seal.

### Lower Locks

Massive lock gates divide the headwater into controlled channels. Workers use
Water creatures and mechanical winches together; neither is treated as a
replaceable tool. During the crisis, several gates anticipate commands that no
keeper issued, creating safe but eerily rigid water levels.

The player restores manual control through a traversal puzzle based on opening
multiple valid routes rather than finding one predetermined sequence.

### Basin Forum

The central plaza steps down toward a circular pool whose surface reflects the
sky even in rain. Public decisions are debated here, and every proposal must
include a method for revision. The forum contains the inn, town map, duel
tables, and the first clear view of both the Seal Archive and Upper Spillway.

### Current Market

Terraced stalls sell river tools, waterproof card cases, Glasswater imports,
and Water cards. The specialist card shop emphasizes Liquid People evolution,
draw and bounce tools, Cyber Lords, and patient finishers. Advanced inventory
unlocks after restoring the Water Seal and after earning the Tidal Crest,
rewarding progress in both related locations without making one magically
control the other.

### Pilgrim Quay

Small boats carry keepers across the inner basin. They are visible world detail
and optional shortcuts, not timed transportation. The quay also hosts practice
duels on broad floating platforms anchored safely away from the locks.

### Keeper Steps

Residences and training rooms climb the western terraces. Apprentice keepers
study how incomplete information changes responsible decisions. Their lessons
contrast sharply with Glasswater's corrupted prediction engine: uncertainty is
something to manage honestly, not eliminate by taking more data.

### Seal Archive

The archive stores consent records, calibration histories, and disagreements
between generations of keepers. No record is presented as an infallible oracle.
Corrections remain beside original entries so later readers can see why a model
changed.

The Curator has inserted pristine counterfeit records with no corrections,
missing witnesses, and conclusions that never admit another possible route.
Comparing these records with Glasswater's recovered ledger identifies which
parts of the Water pattern were derived from stolen memories.

### Reflection Walk

A quiet path circles natural pools below the archive. Authentic card echoes
show several plausible continuations; corrupted pools repeat one flawless
outcome regardless of who approaches. Optional NPC scenes with Marin and Garran
let the player compare analysis, caution, and consent without declaring either
information or instinct inherently virtuous.

### Upper Spillway Ward

The northern ward catches rain and snowmelt from Stormbreak. Tall spillway walls,
weather vanes, and covered bridges visually join the water town to the plateau.
The Rainstep Gate provides a permanent outer-tier route to Stormbreak once the
relay network opens.

## Water Resonance Sanctuary

The sanctuary lies behind the Seal Archive inside a natural cavern where five
streams enter the basin. Its central mechanism records how informed choices
preserve the ability to change course. Healthy reflections branch and recombine;
the corrupted Seal collapses every reflection into the Curator's preferred
future.

Restoration has three parts:

1. Recover the consent ledger from Glasswater's Prediction Hall.
2. Restore manual control at the Lower Locks so multiple physical routes remain
   viable.
3. Remove the counterfeit archive entries while preserving legitimate records
   that were voluntarily contributed.

The final decision is not “keep all data” or “destroy all data.” The player and
keepers separate consented records from stolen ones, document uncertainty, and
recalibrate the Seal using several living choices made in the present.

## Story and duel structure

Tidevault contains no Circuit Crest. Its main challenge is the Water Seal story,
supported by ordinary town duelists and a senior Seal keeper. Neris may visit
after the Tidal Crest match, but a new Tidevault character must lead the local
arc rather than allowing Glasswater's Crest Holder to own both towns.

Suggested duel identities include:

- A lock worker using Water/Nature mana and bounce
- An archive examiner using Cyber Lords and careful card selection
- A quay duelist using Liquid People evolution
- A weather keeper using Water/Light blockers and tap effects
- The senior keeper using a late-game Water control deck that rewards keeping
  multiple lines of play available

The senior keeper's reward should be a powerful Water card distinct from Neris's
Crystal Paladin reward. The town also offers enough Liquid People and utility
cards to make Water decks viable without buying every card in Glasswater.

## Anchor NPCs

- **Selka Rill, senior Seal keeper:** Water/Nature control with Legendary Bynor
  as her signature card. Selka leads the local story and refuses to let Neris or
  any visitor decide Tidevault's response alone.
- **Daro Venn, lockmaster:** Water/Nature mana and bounce. He restores manual
  pressure controls with Garran and hosts practical lock-yard duels.
- **Ione Pell, archive examiner:** Cyber Lords, card selection, and hand
  disruption. She identifies records that are too perfect to be authentic.
- **Merrit Blue, quay captain:** Liquid People evolution and tempo. Merrit runs
  the floating practice platforms and a late-game rematch ladder.
- **Ossa Rain, weather keeper:** Water/Light blockers and tapping effects. She
  maintains the Rainstep gauges shared with Stormbreak.

## Story-state changes

### Before the outer network opens

- Tidevault can be seen or mentioned through Glasswater maps and sealed relay
  notices, but cannot be reached.
- Tidevault readings arrive at Glasswater with unexplained perfect predictions.
- The pressure checkpoint remains visibly closed for a physical reason.

### During the Water Seal crisis

- Lock gates move before workers signal them.
- Reflections show the same outcome to different people.
- Archive staff disagree over deleting the corrupted records.
- The main routes, inn, shop, and exits remain usable.

### After restoring the Water Seal

- Basin reflections once again show several plausible routes.
- Manual and automatic lock commands agree.
- The market adds advanced Water cards.
- Glasswater receives trustworthy current readings without private match data.
- Tideglass Aqueduct and Rainstep Traverse remain open for backtracking.

### During Act III

- Blank patches cross the basin without erasing its whole color palette.
- Keepers use handwritten observations when enchanted gauges become unreliable.
- Restored archive records provide one of the five standards used to enter the
  Hollow Archive.

## Visual direction

- Dark slate roofs over pale stone walls with blue ceramic channels
- Animated water below walkways, spillway foam, rain chains, and pressure wheels
- Blue lamps reflected in stepped pools rather than tropical harbor imagery
- Wide bridges and terraces that remain readable beneath foreground spray
- A visual gradient from Glasswater masonry at the south gate to Stormbreak
  stone and wind equipment at the north gate

## Implementation notes

- Add Tidevault as a planned `town` region only when its exterior is painted.
- Keep the Water Seal as story metadata, not a collision or tile-layer concept.
- Use `Tideglass Aqueduct` for the Glasswater route and `Rainstep Traverse` for
  the Stormbreak route.
- Gate both approaches through the same Confluence-era progression condition;
  once opened, neither route closes.
- Reserve stable IDs such as `tidevault`, `tideglass_aqueduct`, and
  `rainstep_traverse` when implementation begins.
