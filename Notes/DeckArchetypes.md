All stronger NPC decks will fall into the following archetypes:

Three primary archetypes: Rush, Tempo (midrange), and Control. There is also an additional Gimmick archetype.

Diamond Cutter: Light decks that use big blockers, and then diamond cutter as a finisher. Control deck.

Fire Humans: Rush deck with Balbaro, Valdios, additionally perhaps Valkaizer and Gandaval

Water Liquid People: Midrange deck with Crystal Paladin/Lancer/Spinslicer/Jouster.

Mana Nexus deck: Add shield trigger to shields

Beast Folk: Rush with Barkwhip, Fighter Dual Fang, additionally perhaps Storm Wrangler, Earth Ripper

Dragons: Either mono fire, or FN, FD, FL, FW 

Survivors: Typically midrange, with or without evo

Wave strikers: Rush/midrange

Light Blocker Wall: Control deck. Cards like Mist Rias, Phal Eega.

Dark Hand Discard: Horrid Worm, Ghost Touch etc.
